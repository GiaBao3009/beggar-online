# Character asset attribution

The character's anatomical head is adapted from **Lee Perry-Smith / LPS Head**,
a head scan by Lee Perry-Smith, © I-R Entertainment Ltd.

- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
- License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Original archive: https://casual-effects.com/data/
- Archive license record: https://casual-effects.com/g3d/data10/research/model/lpshead/info.js
- GLB, source color map, and tangent normal map: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith
- Archive conversions by Morgan McGuire and Guedis Cardenas, Williams College.

Changes in this application: repositioned and cropped geometry, aged facial
proportions, expression morph targets, desaturated/aged albedo with additional
wrinkles and age spots, and a supplemental wrinkle-height texture.
The hairstyle, facial-hair strands, clothing, hands, and character animation
are application-specific procedural geometry and are not part of the original scan.

Clothing albedo, OpenGL normal map, and roughness map are adapted from
**Fabric Pattern 07**, Poly Haven, CC0:
https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_pattern_07
The albedo is desaturated, recolored, and weathered for this character.
